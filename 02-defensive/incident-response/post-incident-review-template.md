## 3. Review Questions
- What happened, and in what sequence?
- How was it detected? Could it have been detected sooner?
- What worked well?
- What did not work well?
- Were the plan, playbooks, and tools adequate?
- Were roles and communication clear?
- Was information available when needed?
- What would we do differently?
- What indicators or precursors did we miss?
