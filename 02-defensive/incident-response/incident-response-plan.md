## 5. Severity Classification
| Level | Definition | Examples | Response Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| SEV1 Critical | Business-critical impact, confirmed data breach | Ransomware, active exfiltration | Immediate |
| SEV2 High | Significant impact | Compromised privileged account | 1 h |
| SEV3 Medium | Limited impact | Single-host malware | 4 h |
| SEV4 Low | Minimal impact | Policy violation | 24 h |

## 6. Incident Response Lifecycle (NIST SP 800-61)
### 6.1 Preparation
Tooling, training, contact lists, playbooks, logging, backups.
### 6.2 Detection & Analysis
Sources, validation, scoping, severity assignment, timeline.
### 6.3 Containment
Short-term vs long-term, isolation decisions, evidence preservation first.
### 6.4 Eradication
Remove malware, close access, patch, reset credentials.
### 6.5 Recovery
Restore, validate, monitor, staged return to production.
### 6.6 Post-Incident Activity
Lessons learned, reporting, control improvements.
