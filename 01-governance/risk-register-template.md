## 1. Purpose
Central record of identified risks, their ratings, treatment decisions, and status.

## 2. Rating Scales
**Likelihood:** 1 Rare · 2 Unlikely · 3 Possible · 4 Likely · 5 Almost certain
**Impact:** 1 Negligible · 2 Minor · 3 Moderate · 4 Major · 5 Severe
**Score = Likelihood x Impact**

| Score | Level | Required Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1-4 | Low | Accept / monitor |
| 5-9 | Medium | Treat within planned cycle |
| 10-15 | High | Treat with priority, management sign-off |
| 16-25 | Critical | Immediate action, executive escalation |

## 5. Review Cadence
- Critical/High: monthly · Medium: quarterly · Low: annually
- Re-assess after major incidents, changes, or audits.

## 6. Risk Acceptance
Accepted risks require owner + executive sign-off, an expiry date, and are tracked here.
